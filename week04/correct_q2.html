Week 04 - Question 02

🎉 CORRECT!

The correct answer is Yellowish-green plumage with black and white wing markings..

According to the FRIM publication Simbiosis Flora & Fauna, the common iora, scientifically known as Aegithina tiphia, has yellowish-green plumage with black and white markings on its wings.

🌿 INTERESTING FACT

DID YOU KNOW?

The common iora has a melodious call. It can be found in open forests, plantations, gardens and coastal areas.
