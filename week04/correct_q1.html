Week 04 - Question 01

🎉 CORRECT!

The correct answer is Frequency of tidal flooding.

Mangrove forest zones are classified according to the frequency of tidal flooding. Tidal influence affects the environmental conditions and distribution of mangrove species in different zones.

🌿 INTERESTING FACT

DID YOU KNOW?

Mangrove forests grow in coastal environments influenced by tides. Different levels of tidal flooding create distinct conditions for mangrove vegetation.
