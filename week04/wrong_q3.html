Week 04 - Question 03

❌ WRONG!

The correct answer is 25 metres.

According to the FRIM publication Hutan Paya Laut Sungai Kuantan, Intsia bijuga, commonly known as merbau ipil, is a small to medium-sized tree that can reach a maximum height of 25 metres.

🌿 INTERESTING FACT

DID YOU KNOW?

Merbau ipil has a straight trunk with finely cracked bark. Its flowers are large and white, while its compound leaves are another characteristic feature of this tree.
