The correct answer is 10 metres.

According to the FRIM publication Ulaman & Herba Masakan di FRIM: Amalan Tradisi & Khasiat Ubatan, Salix babylonica, also known as janda merana, can grow to a maximum height of 10 metres.

🌿 INTERESTING FACT

DID YOU KNOW?

The inner bark of the tree is believed to be toxic and may cause skin inflammation, red blood cell breakdown (haemolysis), and kidney damage.
