LIBRARY WEEKLY QUIZ

Week 02 - Question 01

🎉 CORRECT!

According to the FRIM publication Ulaman & Herba Masakan di FRIM: Amalan Tradisi & Khasiat Ubatan, Paederia foetida L. (sekentut) is listed among the plants traditionally used to help relieve digestive discomfort.

Its traditional uses include relieving bloating, excessive gas in the body and intestinal discomfort.

🌿 INTERESTING FACT

FRIENDLY REMINDER!

Avoid eating daun sekentut before attending public gathering, as it may stimulate flatulence and lead to some rather embarrassing moments.
