Week 03 - Question 02

🎉 CORRECT!

The answer is Dark head, red cheeks and metallic green body.

The male ruby-cheeked sunbird (Anthreptes singalensis) is described as having a dark head, red cheeks, orange chest and yellow belly.

🐦 INTERESTING FACT

DID YOU KNOW?

Male and female ruby-cheeked sunbirds have different appearances. The female has a greenish body, while the male has a dark head.
