🎉 CORRECT!

The answer is Mucilage.

The mucilage in okra contains water-soluble fibre (polysaccharides).

🌿 INTERESTING FACT

DID YOU KNOW?

Okra mucilage contains polysaccharides, which are water-soluble fibre. This substance gives okra its characteristic slippery texture when cut or cooked.
