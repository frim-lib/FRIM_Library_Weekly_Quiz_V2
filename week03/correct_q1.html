Week 03 - Question 01

🎉 CORRECT!

According to the FRIM publication Simbiosis Flora & Fauna, Swietenia macrophylla (mahogany) produces large woody fruits containing approximately 70 winged seeds.

These fruits are eaten by several animals, including squirrels, monkeys, lutungs and macaques.

🌿 INTERESTING FACT

DID YOU KNOW?

Each mahogany contains approximately 70 winged seeds.
